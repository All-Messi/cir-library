## 3 John Chapter 1

<p class="MsoNormal" style='margin-bottom:0in;'><span class="verse verse-v2" id="v2"><sup class="verse-num">2</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>I pray that you’ll be as prosperous and healthy in every way as you are in your soul. </span></span>

### Cooperation and Opposition


<p class="MsoNormal" style='margin-bottom:0in;'><span class="verse verse-v5" id="v5"><sup class="verse-num">5</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>Friend<sup>sg</sup>, you’re acting faithfully in what you do for your fellow believers in Christ<sup>Messiah</sup>, especially for the ones you don’t know. </span></span>
<span class="verse verse-v6" id="v6"><sup class="verse-num">6</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>In front of the church, they’ve testified to your love. You do well to send them forward in a way worthy of God. </span></span>
<span class="verse verse-v7" id="v7"><sup class="verse-num">7</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>They went out for The Name, not taking anything from Gentiles. </span></span>
<span class="verse verse-v8" id="v8"><sup class="verse-num">8</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>We should support servants like them and so become their true fellow workers.            </span><b><span style='font-size:10.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>3 Jn 5-8</span></b></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style='margin-bottom:0in;'><span class="verse verse-v9" id="v9"><sup class="verse-num">9</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, who loves to take charge among them, wouldn’t receive us. </span></span>
<span class="verse verse-v10" id="v10"><sup class="verse-num">10</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>So if I come, I’ll remind people how he slanders us, and—more than that—doesn’t receive disciples, stops the ones that want to, and excommunicates them from the church.                                                                                                                                                                                                              </span><b><span style='font-size:10.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>3 Jn 9-10</span></b></span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal" style='margin-bottom:0in;'><span class="verse verse-v11" id="v11"><sup class="verse-num">11</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>Imitate good, not evil. Whoever does what’s good belongs to God; whoever does what’s evil hasn’t seen him. </span></span>
<span class="verse verse-v12" id="v12"><sup class="verse-num">12</sup> <span style='font-size:12.0pt;line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>Demetrius receives good testimony from everybody, from the truth itself, and from us. You know you can rely on what we say.     </span><b><span style='font-size:10.0pt; line-height:115%;font-family:"Times New Roman",serif'>3 Jn 11-12</span></b></span></p>
